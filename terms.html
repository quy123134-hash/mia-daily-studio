Mia Daily Studio - Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. About the app

Mia Daily Studio is a personal content tool used by its owner to create short lifestyle vlog videos and send finished videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The app is not offered to the public and is not sold.

2. Use of TikTok

The app connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and only after the account owner signs in and grants permission. Videos are uploaded to the account's TikTok drafts (inbox). Nothing is published automatically: the account owner reviews, edits and posts each video inside the TikTok app.

3. Content responsibility

The account owner is responsible for the videos uploaded through the app and for following TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including labeling AI-generated content where required.

4. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to show which account is connected, and video upload (video.upload) to send videos to drafts. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions).

5. No warranty

The app is provided as is, for personal use, without any warranty.
