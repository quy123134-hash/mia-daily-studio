Mia Daily Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

Mia Daily Studio is a personal content tool run by a single creator on their own computer. It is used only with the creator's own TikTok account.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

After the account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission, the app receives:

• basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) to show which account is connected;

• an access token that allows uploading videos to that account's drafts.

The app does not access followers, messages, comments, likes or any other user's data.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload videos created by the account owner to their own TikTok drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not sent to any third party. They are deleted when the account is disconnected.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

6. Your choices

The account owner can disconnect the app at any time in the app, or revoke access in TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions). Disconnecting removes the stored tokens.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available at this address.
